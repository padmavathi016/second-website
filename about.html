OUR STORY

Preserving the Beauty of Indian Music

Paddhu Music Academy was created with a passion for preserving the richness of Indian music and making quality musical education accessible to everyone.

Our academy brings together traditional techniques, experienced mentors and practical performance training to help students discover their own musical voice.
